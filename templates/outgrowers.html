{% wm_icon "outgrower" size=18 %} Buy-Back Conditions

  • We buy 'PURE' New Zealand White & Californian rabbits ONLY
  • Rabbits must be 3 months old & weigh 2.1–2.4 kg live weight
  • All rabbits will be thoroughly inspected for quality
  • We do NOT buy produce from non-outgrowers
  • Deliveries must be pre-arranged 48 hours in advance
  • Receiving bay open: 8:30am–11:30am (Monday–Friday)
  • Dispatch bay open: All day (Thursday–Friday)
  • Delivery quantities must match expected cage output
