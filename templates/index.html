Who we are

A complete white-meat ecosystem, built in Harare

The White Meat Company was born from a bold vision to commercialise rabbit farming and position Zimbabwe as a regional powerhouse in the white-meat value chain. We combine local expertise with modern technique across breeding stock, international-standard cages, expert-led Masterclass training and a guaranteed market for our farmers — all the way through to The Rabbit Hole Bar & Grill.

What we offer
