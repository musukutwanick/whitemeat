What is the masterclass?

Comprehensive, professional training

Our Rabbit Farming Masterclass is an intensive programme designed to equip you with all the knowledge, skills and practical experience needed to establish and operate a successful rabbit farming business.

Developed from over 7 years of commercial rabbit farming experience, it combines theory with hands-on practice, business planning and guaranteed market access — whether you're a complete beginner or scaling an existing operation.
